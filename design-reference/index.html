A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What can I do in the browser studio?

Upload a video, set clip boundaries, edit captions, change the frame format and export a rendered clip. Export runs in your browser, so keep the editor open while it records your selected segment.

Does ForgeClip select highlights automatically?

Automatic highlights and transcription use the AI services connected to your workspace. The app displays their connection status. You can edit clips manually while those services are being configured.

Where do my videos go?

Local editing keeps your video on this device. Cloud storage and processing send it to your connected backend only when you choose those actions.

Which video formats can I upload?

Use a video format your browser can play. MP4 with H.264 is the most widely supported. Available export formats depend on your browser’s recording capabilities.

Can I use ForgeClip on my phone?

The website and editor adapt to mobile screens. Rendering longer clips works best on a desktop. The cinematic experience preserves the supplied landscape compositions on phones.

How do subscriptions work?

Plans and checkout are provided by the billing service connected to your workspace. ForgeClip shows live plans when that connection is available and never simulates a completed purchase.
